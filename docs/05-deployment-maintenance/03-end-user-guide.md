# End-User Guide & FAQ — My Vehicle Spend

Welcome! This guide helps you track every rupee your car or bike costs, and keep an eye on your vehicle loan.

---

## 1. Getting started

### 1.1 Create your account
1. Open the app link on your phone or computer.
2. Tap **Sign up**. Enter your email and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. Check your email and confirm (if asked). Check the spam folder if you can't see the email.

### 1.2 Install the app on your phone (recommended)
- **Android (Chrome):** tap ⋮ menu → **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** tap the **Share** button → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Computer (Chrome/Edge):** click the install icon in the address bar.

The app opens like any other app and works with a weak network.

### 1.3 Add your vehicle
1. Tap **More → Vehicles → +**.
2. Choose **Car**, **Bike**, **Scooter** or **Other**.
3. Type your registration number (for example `MH12AB1234`). Spaces are optional.
4. Choose fuel type and enter the odometer reading shown on the meter today.
5. Tap **Save**.

You can add more vehicles later and switch between them from the top of the Home screen.

## 2. Logging expenses

### 2.1 Quick add (fastest)
Tap **➕** → **Expense**, then:
1. Pick a category (for example **Tolls & Parking → FASTag recharge**).
2. Enter the amount.
3. Tap **Save**.

Date, vehicle and payment mode default to today, your active vehicle and UPI. Change them only if needed.

### 2.2 Full expense form
Add more detail when you have time: vendor, odometer, GST, notes and up to 3 receipt photos.

**Tip:** The GST amount is shown on most garage bills as "GST incl." — type that part in the GST box to see the tax you paid.

### 2.3 Fuel fill-up
Tap **➕ → Fuel**:
- Enter the odometer reading (from the meter).
- Enter litres and price per litre. The total is worked out for you.
- Tick **Full tank** if you filled to the top. This is what makes mileage accurate.

Fuel fill-ups are automatically counted as expenses too. You do not need to add them twice.

### 2.4 Common categories at a glance
| If you paid for… | Category to choose |
|---|---|
| Petrol, diesel, CNG | Fuel & Energy |
| Home EV charging | Fuel & Energy › EV charging (home) |
| Motor insurance premium | Insurance |
| PUC test certificate | Government & RTO › PUC certificate |
| Road tax, RC fee, transfer | Government & RTO |
| FASTag top-up | Tolls & Parking › FASTag recharge |
| Oil change, service | Maintenance & Service |
| Challan / traffic fine | Traffic Fines |
| Car loan EMI | Loan & Finance › EMI payment (or use **EMI paid** in the Loans screen) |

## 3. Reminders

Never miss a renewal again.

1. Tap **➕ → Reminder**.
2. Choose the type (Insurance, PUC, Licence, Service…).
3. Enter the due date (or due kilometres for service).
4. Choose how many days before to be reminded (default 15).
5. Save.

On the Home screen, **Due soon** lists items coming up. When you renew:
- Tap **Mark done**.
- Enter the amount you paid. The expense is recorded for you.

## 4. Mileage and cost per km

- The app shows **km/l** using full-tank fill-ups.
- It needs at least **two** full-tank fill-ups to show a figure.
- **Cost per km** = total spend ÷ distance driven in the chosen period. It's a great way to compare car vs bike.

## 5. Loan tracker

### 5.1 Add a loan
1. Go to **Loans → +**.
2. Enter the lender, loan amount, interest rate, tenure in months and start date.
3. The app calculates your EMI. If your bank shows a different EMI, type it in — the schedule will adjust.

### 5.2 Record your EMI payment
- Open the loan → find the due instalment → tap **Mark paid**.
- Enter the date and any late fee or bounce charge.
- The EMI is added to your expenses automatically (with the interest part shown separately).

### 5.3 Prepayment and foreclosure
- **Simulate prepayment:** enter an amount and date. Choose:
  - **Reduce tenure** — same EMI, loan ends sooner, you save more interest.
  - **Reduce EMI** — same end date, lower monthly payment.
- **Foreclosure estimate:** shows the amount to pay to close the loan today, including the foreclosure charge your lender has specified.

> The simulator is for planning. Always confirm final figures with your lender.

## 6. Reports and exports

- **Reports** tab: choose a period (This month, Last month, This FY, Last 12 months or custom).
- **Export CSV:** opens in Excel or Google Sheets. Use this for your records or to share with an accountant.
- **Financial year:** Indian FY runs **1 April to 31 March**. Use **This FY** to see totals since 1 April.

---

## 7. Backup and your data

- **Settings → Backup → Export JSON** saves everything to a file. Do this once a month.
- To move to a new phone: sign in on the new phone, then **Settings → Restore**, and choose the JSON file.
- Your data is private. Only you can see it.

---

## 8. Working offline

- You can open the app and view your data without internet.
- New entries are saved on your phone. A badge shows how many are waiting.
- When you're back online, they sync automatically (on iPhone, open the app to trigger sync).

---

## 9. Account and privacy

- **Change password:** Settings → Account → Change password.
- **Sign out:** Settings → Sign out.
- **Delete account:** Settings → Delete account. Type `DELETE` to confirm. This permanently removes all your vehicles, expenses, loans, reminders and receipts. This cannot be undone — export a backup first.

## 10. FAQ

**Q1. Is the app free?**
Yes. There are no subscriptions or ads.

**Q2. Is my data safe?**
Your data is stored in a secure database and is only visible to your account. Receipts are private. We never sell your data.

**Q3. Why does mileage show "insufficient data"?**
Mileage needs at least two full-tank fill-ups. Log your next fill-up with **Full tank** ticked.

**Q4. My EMI in the app is ₹1 or ₹2 different from my bank.**
Banks round the final instalment and sometimes charge slightly different interest. Use the **Record actual payment** option and enter what you actually paid — the schedule will use the bank's figure from that point.

**Q5. I paid the insurance premium but the app still says "due".**
Open the reminder and tap **Mark done**. Enter the premium and the new expiry date.

**Q6. Can I track my family's cars?**
Not in version 1.0. Family sharing is planned for a future version.

**Q7. Can the app read my bank SMS or UPI payments?**
No. Everything is entered manually or by you using receipts. This keeps your bank data private.

**Q8. I'm not receiving reminder notifications.**
Go to **Settings → Reminders → Turn on notifications** and allow notifications in your browser or phone settings.

**Q9. The app shows a "New version available" message. What should I do?**
Tap **Refresh**. Your data is safe.

**Q10. I entered something wrong. Can I fix it?**
Yes. Open the entry and tap **Edit**. Deleted entries are kept for 30 days on the server and can be restored by contacting the administrator (in-app restore is a v1.1 item).

**Q11. Is tax advice included?**
No. The app records your amounts and gives you exports. For tax questions, consult a Chartered Accountant.

**Q12. Why is the rupee amount shown as ₹1,23,456 and not ₹123,456?**
That's the Indian number format (lakhs and crores). The value is the same.
